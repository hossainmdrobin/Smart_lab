## Source of truth

`docs/CLIENT_REQUIREMENTS.md` is the authoritative requirements document. Nothing in it may be modified without explicit client approval. When the client requirements are silent, treat the item as a **placeholder** and mark it clearly.

## What must never be fabricated

- Laboratory founding year, history, or establishment date
- Abbreviation, logos, or institutional relationships
- Team members, leadership structure, roles, or affiliations
- Publications, DOIs, journal details, or author lists
- Statistics, awards, rankings, or claims such as "world-leading" or "internationally renowned"
- Therapeutic areas, software expertise, or experimental capabilities not confirmed by the laboratory
- Street addresses or map locations
- Unapproved university or partner logos
- Predicted compounds presented as proven medicine; computation alone must never be presented as establishing clinical effectiveness

## Placeholder policy

Any information not supplied by the laboratory must be stored as a clearly identified placeholder in the CMS and rendered as such on the public site. Placeholders must:

- Be visually distinct (e.g., "Content pending", "To be confirmed")
- Never appear in published content unless explicitly marked as draft
- Be removed or replaced with approved content before launch

## Target audiences

- Academic researchers and potential collaborators
- Prospective research students and research associates
- Universities and research institutions
- Funding organizations
- Scientific and industry partners

## Tone and positioning

The site must feel like a credible scientific laboratory website. It must **not** resemble:

- A pharmaceutical sales website
- A hospital website
- A wet laboratory website
- A generic software company website

Use restrained, academic language. Avoid marketing hyperbole.
